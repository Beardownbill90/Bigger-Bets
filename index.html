WELCOME TO BIGGER BETS

YOUR FIRST FIVE MINUTES.

  1. Start with 1,000 free coins. Open your free starter pack.
  2. Equip cards in Collection. One team, one player, one stadium.
  3. Pick a real upcoming matchup. Choose a winner, enter a coin amount, and see the possible return before confirming. Picks close at the scheduled start.
  4. Wait for the real final result. Your pick automatically settles when ESPN reports the final result; checks run every 15 seconds while open and when you return. Player props wait for confirmed final stats. A win returns your stake plus profit. Soccer offers a draw selection. A canceled game or a tie in a two-result sport refunds your stake.
  5. Game of the Day. The marked matchup doubles your winning profit, including card bonuses. Your stake is returned once.
  6. Grow your collection. Use coins on packs. Three qualifying verified wins earn a free Standard pack, up to one per day. Each real game counts once; minimum 25-coin entry and 80% or lower model chance. Manual results do not qualify. You can add free practice coins anytime.

Team and player cards match the team you pick. Stadium cards match the venue name across leagues, whichever result you pick. Bonuses affect winning profit only, capped at 40%. Picks use a snapshot of your equipped cards.
