WELCOME TO BIGGER BETS

YOUR FIRST FIVE MINUTES.

  1. Start with 1,000 free coins. Open your free starter pack.
  2. Equip cards in Collection. One team, one player, one stadium.
  3. Pick a real upcoming matchup. Choose a winner, enter a coin amount, and see the possible return before confirming. Picks close at the scheduled start.
  4. Wait for the real final result. Your pick stays pending until ESPN reports the final result. A win returns your stake plus profit. Soccer offers a draw selection. A canceled game or a tie in a two-result sport refunds your stake.
  5. Game of the Day. The marked matchup doubles your winning profit, including card bonuses. Your stake is returned once.
  6. Grow your collection. Use coins on packs. Every third win earns a free Standard pack. You can add free practice coins anytime.

Team and player cards match the team you pick. Stadium cards match the venue name across leagues, whichever result you pick. Bonuses affect winning profit only, capped at 40%. Picks use a snapshot of your equipped cards.
